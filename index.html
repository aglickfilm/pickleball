    The court

    The court is badminton-sized: 20 × 44 ft. Teams of two.
    The kitchen is the 7-ft zone on each side of the net. No volleying from in there.
    The serve goes underhand and crosscourt, landing past the kitchen.

    Rules to relish

    Serve underhand, crosscourt

    Hit below your waist from behind the baseline. Aim diagonally, past the kitchen. You get one try.

    Let it bounce twice

    The return of serve must bounce, and so must the next shot back. After those two bounces, volleys are fair game.

    Stay out of the kitchen

    No volleying while you're in the kitchen or on its line. You can step in to play a ball that has bounced.

    Play to 11, win by 2

    Only the serving team can score a point. Lose a rally on your serve and the serve moves on.

    Calling the score

    Before each serve, the server calls three numbers:

    4servers2receivers1server #

    Server # is 1 or 2, for the first or second partner to serve. The first game starts at 0-0-2, so the first team gets one server only.

    A rally ends when…

    The ball goes out or into the net, bounces twice, or someone volleys from the kitchen. A ball on the line is in, except a serve on the kitchen line.
